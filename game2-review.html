The initial jump tests 40 before the body. Each pass adds 3 to %eax and arithmetically shifts the positive %edi right by 2: (40, 2) → (10, 5) → (2, 8) → (0, 11). The last test is not greater than zero, so the loop stops. Your A stops one iteration early.
